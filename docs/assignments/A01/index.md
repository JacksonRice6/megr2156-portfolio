## Analyze
For my first portfolio I am analyzing I chose: https://github.com/NateKarau61/Engineering-Portfolio

 
**Navigability**

This portfolio is extremely easy to navigate. You are immediately met with all the projects in order and numbered when you open the page. Upon clicking each project you can see the drawings, pictures, and a presentation for the project. Each drawing and picture is neatly labeled, letting you know exactly what it is. The only problem is you do not know what the project is until you click on it because it is labeled "Project01". You have to go to the README page to find what each project is. Other than that, very easy to navigate.

**Reproducibility**

In order to reproduce this, the most important part would be the drawings. Each drawing is labeled as to what it is and each one has clear dimensions, they are not all pushed together and point to what they are dimensioning. Each drawing has three distinct views(when needed), right, top, front. It also has a drawing of the full assembly. However, based on the assembly drawings, from what I can see, it would be difficult to understand how to assemble it without having to guess at a few things.

**Evidence of Reasoning**

This portfolio does not show how the decisions were made or why they were made. It shows the final product, however, it also includes steps to building the product.

**Professional Tone**

There is very limited words in this portfolio. Where there are words though are very concise and clear. They let you know exactly what they are talking about with limited amount of words, which is, in my opinion, the best way to convey information.
